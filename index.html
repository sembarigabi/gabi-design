Work with me

Three ways
to work together

I work with small wellbeing businesses and creators who have the expertise but need someone to help make it real. Everything starts with a conversation.

01
Always free
The discovery call
Tell me what you're building
We talk for 45 minutes. You tell me about your business, where you want to go, what's feeling stuck. No pitch, no agenda from my side — just genuine curiosity.

At the end, if I spot a real opportunity we're both excited about, we explore working together. If not, you leave with clarity and a fresh perspective. Either way, nothing to lose.
02
Co-production · revenue share + production fee

Let's make
something.

You have the expertise, the audience, and the idea. I bring the execution — filming, editing, illustrations, pricing strategy, packaging, customer infrastructure. Whatever the product needs that you can't or don't want to do alone.

We agree on a production fee and a revenue percentage. Your name and mine on what we make together.

What this has looked like
🎬
Yoga video bundle — concept, filming, editing, pricing strategy, customer service during launch
📓
Tarot exercise journal — co-writing, illustrations, project framing, print production
✦
Could also be: a course, a digital product, a membership, a workshop series — if the idea is solid, we find a way
03
Fixed fee · by request
Customer journey deep-dive
For businesses already earning
A focused analysis of your customer journey — where people drop off, what stops them buying, what would make them come back. Delivered as a clear, prioritised action report with concrete next steps.

This is for businesses already generating revenue who want to grow it intentionally. Not the right fit if you're just starting out — and I'll tell you that honestly.
